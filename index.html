About

Bridging power engineering, cloud, and AI.

Song Zhang (IEEE S'13 M'14 SM'20) is a recognized leader at the intersection of power systems, cloud technology, and artificial intelligence. He currently serves as Supervisor, Grid AI Innovation at ISO New England, where he spearheads the Grid AI Innovation roadmap — identifying and prioritizing business use cases across operational functions, building a high-performing team of AI engineers and data scientists, and fostering a culture of continuous learning and innovation to enhance operational efficiency and strategic planning. He brings more than a decade of experience in power systems engineering, cloud-based grid analytics, and AI applications for transmission operations and planning.

Prior to his current role, Song served as Head of Grid Analytics Intelligence at GridUnity, and before that as Grid Solution Lead at Amazon Web Services (Energy & Utilities IBU), where he partnered with utilities and grid operators on modernization strategies spanning AI, cloud computing, and advanced power system analytics. He also served earlier as R&D Lead Analyst and Cloud CoE Lead at ISO New England, leading cloud adoption initiatives and owning cloud application and infrastructure design for large-scale planning studies. Earlier, he contributed to energy management and grid automation technologies at Alstom Grid (now a GE Vernova company) from 2014 to 2017.

His technical expertise spans power system planning studies, synchrophasor-based control systems, cloud-native simulation environments, and scalable AI architectures for grid modernization. Across his career, Song has pioneered the convergence of AI, cloud-native platforms, and power grid modernization — driving measurable transformation across the energy sector and directly influencing how utilities adopt cloud and AI to improve reliability, reduce costs, and accelerate the clean energy transition.

Dr. Zhang has authored 30+ peer-reviewed IEEE publications with 1,300+ citations on grid modernization, cloud computing in power systems, and AI-enabled utility operations — including recent work focused on the intersection of AI, cloud infrastructure, and transmission system modernization. He holds a patent, and has received multiple IEEE PES Best Paper Awards and ISO New England CEO Innovation Awards.
